The companion changes; the resource doesn't

September 2026

  • Added classroom need based discovery: What are you trying to improve?
  • Added the Inclusive Practice lens across the 144 strategies.
  • Added About the evidence.
  • Added professional learning support, with a 30 minute activity on every guide.
  • Added Something to try tomorrow.
  • Added further reading and evidence to all six guides.
  • Added an individual page for every one of the 144 strategies, each with why you might use it, an example, a way to begin and what to notice. Where a tool type genuinely helps, the page says how; a handful carry an AI prompt shaped by the STAIR approach.
  • Reorganised the homepage and navigation around three ways in: what you're trying to improve, something to try tomorrow and exploring the 144. The story behind the guides moved to About Pedagogy First, every strategy page gained a breadcrumb trail, and the search now also matches classroom needs and inclusive practice terms. No content was removed and no addresses changed.
