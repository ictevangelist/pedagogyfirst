Pedagogy First. Technology Second.

Four questions, in this order

1
The learning

What do my pupils need to learn, and where are they now?

Start with the need in front of you.

2
The pedagogy

Which evidence‑informed approach fits that need?

Retrieval, formative assessment, feedback, questioning, explanations, metacognition.

3
The technology

Will a tool support teaching, enhance learning or reduce workload?

Yes: use it, on purpose.
No: leave it out.

4
The impact

What actually happened?

Keep it, adapt it or drop it.

With AI

Use it to help manage the activity, but never the struggle for learners. Check its work, like every child in your classroom.
