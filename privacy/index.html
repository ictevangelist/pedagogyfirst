Privacy

Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how to turn analytics off.

The short version

What this site collects

Very little. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no forms and no comments, and I never ask for or want information about pupils. Your reading-controls choices (text size, contrast, spacing) and your analytics choice are kept in your own browser, on your device, and are never sent anywhere.

Google Analytics

Counting visits, for statistics only

I use Google Analytics to count visits and improve the site: how many people visit, which pages get read and roughly where in the world they're reading from. It is used only for statistics. Nothing is used for advertising: advertising features are switched off and no data is shared for ads.

Google Analytics sets its own cookies (_ga and one starting _ga_) so that repeat visits can be counted. The data goes to Google, which processes it on my behalf. What I see is aggregated (page views, visit counts, general location, device type) and I can't identify you from it. Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store IP addresses.

Your choice

How to turn it off

Use Turn off at the bottom of any page. Your choice is saved in your browser, on your device only, and you can turn analytics back on in the same place. With JavaScript switched off, analytics never loads at all.
