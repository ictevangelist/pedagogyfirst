Build on Student Responses

The strategy, as published

Exactly as it appears on the card

19 🔁Build on Student Responses

Alexander’s EEF programme showed gains when teachers extended rather than evaluated. Return an answer: Aisha says X. Does anyone want to add or challenge? Conductor, not judge.

Suggested technology live display or response capture tool · Informed by Alexander

Why you might use it

The problem it may help with

When Alexander's dialogic teaching approach was trialled with the EEF, one teacher move stood out, which was extending student responses rather than evaluating them. The card's image is exactly right: conductor, not judge.

Returning an answer to the room, so Aisha says X, does anyone want to add or challenge, does two things at once. It takes the verdict away from you, and it makes the class the audience for each other's thinking rather than just for yours.

What it could look like

One way in your classroom

Discussing a poem's ending, a pupil says it feels hopeful. You return it to the room, saying "Sam thinks the ending is hopeful", and asking who wants to add to that or push against it. Three more contributions arrive before you speak again, and the third one disagrees with the first, politely, with a line from the poem to back it up.

Try it

A way to begin

Choose one returning phrase, such as does anyone want to add or challenge that, and use it in place of your usual evaluation for a single lesson.

What to notice

Reading what happens

  • How long the chains of pupil contributions run before you come back in.
  • Whether pupils start addressing each other by name rather than routing everything through you.
  • What your withheld verdict does to the pupil who answered, because the pause after their answer is wait time 2 in action.

Things to think about

Before and while you use it

  • Conducting is still steering. You choose whose answer gets returned and when to close a line down, so extending isn't the same as stepping back completely.
  • Returning a wrong answer for the class to work on needs the same climate as cold calling, or the pupil hears it as being marked in public.
