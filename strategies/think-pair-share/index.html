Think-Pair-Share

The strategy, as published

Exactly as it appears on the card

09 👥Think-Pair-Share

Lyman’s think-pair-share gives every student thinking time before public exposure. Pairs test ideas with a peer first. The result is more considered and more substantive discussion.

Suggested technology partner chat tool or response system · Informed by Lyman

Find this under Improve classroom discussion · Support access and participation · Inclusive practice: access and participation

Part of Wait Time & Thinking Space in 04 Questioning & Discussion, where it sits alongside its neighbouring strategies, the infographic and the thinking for the whole area.

Why you might use it

The problem it may help with

Whole-class questioning exposes first-draft thinking to public judgement, which is one reason so many students simply decline to offer any. Lyman's think-pair-share changes the sequence, so there's thinking time alone, then a partner to test the idea on, and only then the room.

By the time an idea is spoken publicly it's already been formed and rehearsed once, and the card's claim is a modest one you can see for yourself: discussion becomes more considered and more substantive.

What it might look like

One version of it, in a classroom

Asked to predict what happens to the current when a second bulb is added, pupils think alone for thirty seconds, then compare predictions with a partner, and then the pairs report back on what they agreed and where they split. The teacher hears reasons, because the pairs have already had to give each other reasons.

Try it

A way to begin

Take one question you'd normally throw open tomorrow and run it as think, pair, share instead, with the stages timed and kept short.

What to notice

Reading what happens

  • Contributions from pupils who rarely say anything in open discussion.
  • Whether the reported answers refer back to the pair talk, along the lines of we thought this, but then.
  • What actually happens in the think stage, because if pairs start talking straight away, the individual thinking is being skipped.

Things to think about

Before and while you use it

  • The think stage is the one that needs protecting, because the value of the structure starts with thinking done alone.
  • Writing during the think stage makes it visible and harder to skip, and write before you speak is the natural partner here.

Where this connects

  • 📝 Write Before You Speak Ask students to write before any verbal response is taken. Writing prevents the quickest from foreclosing others’ thinking. The pen democratises the room before the voice takes over. 04 Questioning & Discussion · Wait Time & Thinking Space
  • 💬 Think, Pair, Share: Disciplined Individual thinking first; paired discussion second; nominated response third. Nomination makes the report-back evidence, not just the most confident voice. Without it, the share is optional. 02 Formative Assessment · Elicit Evidence of Learning
  • ⏰ Wait Time 1: After You Ask Rowe found most teachers wait under one second before speaking. Extending to three seconds increases the length and complexity of responses. Silence is thinking, not a gap to fill. 04 Questioning & Discussion · Wait Time & Thinking Space

The wider thinking

Where the evidence sits

Lyman's original structure has barely needed refining over the years, because protected thinking, followed by a low-stakes rehearsal with a partner, followed by the room, still does the job. The card’s attribution stands as published: Lyman.
