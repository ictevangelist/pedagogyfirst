Words and Images Together

The strategy, as published

Exactly as it appears on the card

13 🖼️Words and Images Together

Paivio’s dual coding: the brain processes words and images through separate channels. Design every slide with both: a key visual paired with minimal text. Two routes to the same idea.

Suggested technology dual coded slide design · Informed by Paivio

Find this under Explain difficult ideas · Support access and participation · Inclusive practice: language and explanation

Part of Multimedia & Dual Coding in 05 Explanations & Modelling, where it sits alongside its neighbouring strategies, the infographic and the thinking for the whole area.

Why you might use it

The problem it may help with

Words and images reach the mind through separate channels, and Paivio's dual coding theory says that using both gives the same idea two routes in.

So when it comes to your slides, pair a key visual with minimal text, and make sure both of them are carrying the one idea.

What it could look like

One way in your classroom

For a lesson on trench warfare, your slide holds one cross-section image and just six words of labelling. Your narration does the explaining, and the image and the sparse text give the idea its second route in.

Try it

A way to begin

Choose one text-only slide and find the visual that shows its idea, then cut the text down to the few words the visual can't carry.

What to notice

Reading what happens

  • Whether pupils sketch when they try to recall the idea, and what their sketches keep hold of.
  • Whether the image comes back when the words are tested later on.

Things to think about

Before and while you use it

  • The aim is two routes to the same idea, so an unrelated image doesn't help, it's just clutter wearing the theory's name.
  • Minimal text is part of the theory rather than a style preference, and the redundancy and coherence cards explain what fuller text costs.

Related strategies

Where this connects

  • 🎧 Narrate, Don’t Label Mayer’s modality: people learn better from images with narration than images with text. Record a narrated screencast over a diagram. Let the voice do what text would overload. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Multimedia & Dual Coding
  • 📍 Keep Words Near Images Mayer’s spatial contiguity: place words near the images they describe. Labels sit beside the diagram, not in a separate list. Use slide design to keep related information together. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Multimedia & Dual Coding
  • ❌ Remove the Clutter Mayer’s coherence: remove every word, image and sound that does not support learning. Strip clip art from slides. Cut background music. Every element on screen must earn its place. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Managing Cognitive Load
  • 📦 Concrete Before Abstract Willingham: anchor new ideas to concrete examples first. Use images, video or objects to make the abstract visible before naming it. Move to the abstract once the concrete is secure. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Structuring Explanations
