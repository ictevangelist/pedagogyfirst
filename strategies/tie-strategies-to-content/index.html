Tie Strategies to Content

The strategy, as published

Exactly as it appears on the card

20 💡Tie Strategies to Content

Willingham: strategies do not float free of subjects. Teach each one inside the content it serves, so students learn to self-regulate this topic, not metacognition in the abstract.

Suggested technology subject-specific strategy banks · Informed by Willingham

Part of Strategy Knowledge & Use in 06 Metacognition & Self-Regulation, where it sits alongside its neighbouring strategies, the infographic and the thinking for the whole area.

Why you might use it

The problem it may help with

A strategy taught in the abstract tends to stay there. Willingham's argument is that strategies don't float free of subjects, because self-regulating an essay isn't the same act as self-regulating a proof, and pupils taught "metacognition" in general often can't find it again once they're inside geography.

So each strategy gets taught inside the content it serves. Pupils learn to self-regulate this topic, in this subject, and the strategy arrives already attached to somewhere it works.

What it could look like

One way in your classroom

Rather than sending the class off to a generic study skills session, you teach self-quizzing inside the rivers topic, using rivers vocabulary, and add it to a subject strategy bank, so the class knows which methods suit map skills, which suit case studies and which suit extended writing. The strategy and the content get learned as one thing.

Try it

A way to begin

Take one strategy that's currently taught generically in your school and re-teach it inside the topic where it pays off most in your subject.

What to notice

Reading what happens

  • Whether pupils use the strategy in the subject where they learned it but nowhere else. Transfer is genuinely hard, so expect to teach the strategy again in new territory rather than assuming it travels on its own.
  • Whether the strategy bank actually gets used, or whether it only really exists as a display.

Things to think about

Before and while you use it

  • This makes strategy instruction every department's job rather than a discrete course, and a subject-specific bank is only really credible when the subject's own teachers built it.
