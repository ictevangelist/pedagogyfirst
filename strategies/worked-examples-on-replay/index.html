Worked Examples on Replay

The strategy, as published

Exactly as it appears on the card

21 📝Worked Examples on Replay

Sweller and Cooper: students learn more from worked examples than equivalent problems. Record a screencast of the example so students can pause and study the method at their pace.

Suggested technology screen recording; LMS for sharing · Informed by Sweller & Cooper

Find this under Make modelling clearer

Part of Modelling & Worked Examples in 05 Explanations & Modelling, where it sits alongside its neighbouring strategies, the infographic and the thinking for the whole area.

Why you might use it

The problem it may help with

Sweller and Cooper showed students can learn more from studying worked examples than from solving equivalent problems, but a worked example on the board lasts only as long as the lesson allows.

Recording it changes the arithmetic. A screencast of the method can be paused, rewound and studied at each pupil's own pace, which suits the way novices need to see a process unfold.

What it might look like

One version of it, in a classroom

Working a full solution to a quadratic on screen, a teacher records the process, narration included, and posts it to the class page. That evening one pupil pauses at the factorising step three times; another skims to the final line to check their own working.

Try it

A way to begin

Record the worked example you were going to do anyway: point the approved screen recording tool at it while you teach, and share the result.

What to notice

Reading what happens

  • Where the pauses and rewinds cluster, if your platform shows you.
  • Whether pupils refer back to the method mid-practice, or only check final answers.

Things to think about

Before and while you use it

  • The replay supplements the live modelling; the thinking aloud, the checks and the questions still happen in the room.
  • Pairing recorded examples with practice problems, example then attempt, is where the next card takes this.

Related strategies

Where this connects

  • 🔁 Alternate Examples and Practice Atkinson: alternate worked examples with practice. Display an example on screen, then set a parallel task. Example, try. Example, try. Interleaving builds understanding faster. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Modelling & Worked Examples
  • 🗣 Think Aloud Rosenshine: model the process, not the product. Use a visualiser or screen share to think aloud live. Narrate your decisions as you work. Make invisible expertise visible to all. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Modelling & Worked Examples
  • 🎥 Short Screencasts Record short screencasts with narration over visuals. Mayer: images plus voice outperforms text-heavy slides. Keep each video to one idea. Brevity beats completeness every time. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Multimedia & Dual Coding
  • ➕ Supplement, Never Substitute The EEF: technology works best when it supplements teaching, not replaces it. A screencast works alongside classroom explanation, not instead of it. Supplement, never substitute. 05 Explanations & Modelling · Structuring Explanations

Technology second

Where technology fits

A screen recording made with the approved tool in your setting and shared through your learning platform is the whole setup; the value sits in the replayable method, not in production polish.
