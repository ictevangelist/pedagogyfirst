Schedule Your Own Spacing

The strategy, as published

Exactly as it appears on the card

21 📅Schedule Your Own Spacing

Regulating when you study is a planning act. Teach students to set their own calendar reminders to revisit topics over time, owning the spacing rather than waiting to be told.

Suggested technology calendar reminders for review · Informed by Zimmerman

Find this under Build independence

Part of Digital Cognition in 06 Metacognition & Self-Regulation, where it sits alongside its neighbouring strategies, the infographic and the thinking for the whole area.

Why you might use it

The problem it may help with

Spacing is usually something that's done to pupils, so you build the retrieval schedule and the pupils turn up to it. That works, but it leaves the regulation with you. Deciding when you're going to study is a planning act in itself, and it's one that can be handed over.

Teaching pupils to set their own calendar reminders to revisit topics puts the spacing schedule in their hands, and that's the point where a classroom routine starts becoming a study habit.

What it could look like

One way in your classroom

At the end of a topic, pupils set three reminders on their calendar, one a few days out, one a fortnight out and one a month out. Each reminder names an action rather than a topic, such as "quiz myself on the glossary" or "redraw the diagram from memory". When it fires, the review is ten minutes, and it's retrieval rather than re-reading.

Try it

A way to begin

Do it together in class the first time, and check that the wording of each reminder names something to do rather than something to look at.

What to notice

Reading what happens

  • Whether reminders name actions or just topics. "Revise photosynthesis" fires and gets dismissed, but "self-quiz on the photosynthesis flashcards" fires and gets done much more often.
  • Whether pupils set their own reminders for later topics without being told, because unprompted scheduling is the handover actually happening.
  • What actually happens when a reminder fires. Ask them, because the answer tells you whether the system is real.

Things to think about

Before and while you use it

  • The reminder is only the trigger, and the review it triggers has to be retrieval, or the schedule is just spaced re-reading.
  • Start with a class-set schedule and fade towards pupil-set, just like any other scaffold in this strand.
